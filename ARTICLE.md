# What I Found Interesting

The article, _Software Engineering in the LLM Era_ (July, 2025), by [Stephanie Kirmer](https://towardsdatascience.com/author/s-kirmer/) talks about the current state of LLMs and AI tools in the software engineering industry. What I found most interesting, is that unlike most "AI-hype" articles, Kirmer carefully analyzes how these tools are both useful and, at times, dangerous depending on their use case. She mentions that for senior engineers, LLMs can boost productivity by handling 'scut work' such as tests, docs, boilerplate, etc. However, for juniors, a heavy reliance on these tools can break the learning process, leading them to produce code without developing the underlying judgment and debugging skills they learn by figuring things out without an LLMs help. Finally, I completely agree with her viewpoint that most companies today think that by using these tools they can work "faster," but at the same time if they reduce the junior roles, there will end up being less seniors overall in the future.
